TiPlay source-rate Android WebRTC transport

The redistributed AAR is built from pinned webrtc-sdk/webrtc sources with the
TiPlay source-rate patch, including its post-stop sending-update guard. The build
reuses webrtc-sdk/webrtc-build@06e3410d8f67d08202e26f55225705596b60778e,
its Android environment, GN configuration and three compatibility patches:
add_license_dav1d.patch, android_webrtc_version.patch and fix_mocks.patch.
The legacy publisher-compat.patch is an equivalence reference, not a build input.
This is not the unmodified publisher Maven binary.

BUILD-PROVENANCE.json records source/depot revisions, actual publisher inputs,
patch hashes, four ABIs, focused test identity, AAR SHA-256 and matching symbol
archive identity. The private symbol archive retains four matching unstripped
WebRTC libraries with line tables; it does not supply symbols for old binaries.
